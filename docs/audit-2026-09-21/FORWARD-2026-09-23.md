Diagnostic only. The data is public consolidated-tape one-minute bars for the
24 configured ETFs, labelled `historical_backfill` on `delayed_sip`, not the
deployment's Alpaca IEX feed. Nothing here can authorize anything.

Scripts: `forward_costs.py` (all 24 rule arms, bracket replay under the

the delta is dominated by how differently the two days moved. It has one

far, and that is recorded rather than explained away. Look 1 needs 30
accepted IEX sessions.

## Reading it

1. **The pooled result is the same in and out of sample**: about -3 bps per
   trade at realistic cost, and about -17 at the modelled cost. The catalogue
   as a whole has no edge at realistic cost. Almost all of the gap to the
   modelled cost is the cost assumption, not the signals.
2. **`opening_range_fade` lost on both forward days**: 8 baseline trades,
   -5.2 bps mean. Eight trades cannot overturn 59 or confirm them. It remains
   the best in-sample candidate and the paper incumbent, and its own sealed
   test starts on 24 September.
3. **`vwap_reversion` is flat forward** (+0.02 bps over 44 trades) and flat in
   sample at realistic cost. Its control-adjusted signal has not shown up as
   raw P&L under any exit tested (`exit-walkforward-2026-09-23.json`).
4. **The trend, breakout and momentum families stay negative** in and out of
   sample.

Nothing in this report changes a frozen hypothesis, a gate, a cost constant
or a risk limit.
