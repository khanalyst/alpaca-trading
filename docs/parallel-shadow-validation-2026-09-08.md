At `19:53:28 UTC` all 24 symbols' bar and quote observations were stale and `market_data_ready=false`. Strict real-time readiness remains **NO-GO**.
- **Diagnostic shadow:** the deployed `deploy/shadow.py` wrapper is start-anchored and removes its former fixed post-work sleep. A new immutable 24-arm cohort—12 canonical baselines plus one fixed one-factor variant for each family—is active and advancing without errors. September 8 remains activation-day warm-up with zero decisions, books, trades, fills, and P&L; it is not qualification or performance evidence.
- **Paper lane:** after a flat-state backup and resume, final health is fresh/OK, flat, operator-unpaused, `paused` by `validated_edge_required`, armed, and `waiting_for_proof` with `resolved=null`. It is correct standby behavior, not a qualified pair or active paper strategy.

The target remains broker-free shadow evaluation of one baseline and one one-factor variant for each of 12 families, with at most one proved strategy/variant pair in paper. Cost, risk, proof, false-discovery, held-out, and qualification rules remain unchanged. No actual candidate is qualified: the fixed diagnostic cohort never enters the authorizing ingestion path, and using one of its arms on paper would be a new experiment requiring explicit authority and gate architecture.

## Provider constraints that shape validation

Alpaca's [Paper Trading documentation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading), updated July 7, 2026, says simulated fills use real-time quotes but omit impact, latency slippage, queue position, fees, and dividends; quantity is not checked against displayed depth. Paper fills are operational evidence, not live execution or P&L proof.

Alpaca's [Real-time Stock Data documentation](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/real-time-stock-pricing-data), updated May 18, 2026, recommends streaming over REST polling. Minute bars arrive after the minute; `updatedBars` can carry late corrections roughly 30 seconds later. Retain event time, receipt time, and revision identity rather than silently substituting history.

Alpaca's [Market Data overview](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api), updated July 16, 2026, distinguishes Basic IEX from consolidated SIP and documents a 30-symbol Basic streaming limit. Keep feed identities distinct. Paid coverage is not a profit guarantee and requires a new evidence identity and confirmation.
