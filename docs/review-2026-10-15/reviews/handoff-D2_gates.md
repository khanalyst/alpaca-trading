## What this part does (as implemented)

research/gates.py (4,461 lines) is the policy-neutral acceptance layer used by the strategy factory, edge discovery and the live-shadow ingest. As implemented it: (1) projects raw replay rows to authorising rows. A row needs quote-priced entry and exit on the bound feed and provider, a quote age of 30 s or less, and must not be no_trade or diagnostic (gates.py:444-562). Production rows always take the strict path (577-592). (2) Builds structural floors (915-949) against immutable protocol minimums: backtest 100 trades/30 sessions/30 clusters, shadow 150/30/30, qualification 100/30/30 (114-122). (3) Pairs candidate and baseline rows on comparison_id or vehicle:symbol:session, dropping duplicate keys (1159-1178, 1618-1626). It then runs a one-sided session-cluster sign-flip test with seed 20260728 (1238-1260). This is exact only up to 16 clusters; otherwise it uses 20,000 Monte Carlo draws with p=(e+1)/(N+1) (research/stats.py:243-254, 1277-1307). It adds a 95% one-sided moving-block cluster bootstrap lower bound (block 5 sessions, 4,000 draws; gates.py:105-106, 1262-1265; stats.py:600-605). (4) Builds a content-hashed envelope (unkeyed sha256, 4402-4405) that derives 31 required checks (67-83, 2955-3236). (5) Re-verifies a persisted envelope by recomputing each component from its source rows and then rebuilding the whole envelope (3304-4281, 4284-4399). Other components: sealed qualification window (2049-2331), walk-forward (1906-2046), cost stress (1452-1580), falsification (1777-1887), BY batch binding (166-365), retirement (1054-1127).

## Summary

- The 25 bps cost-stress veto (gates.py:153-154, 1551) requires a mean gross edge of about 25 bps per trade. A +2 bps-net trade ends at -10 bps stressed expectancy (executed), so this check alone blocks every realistic intraday ETF edge (HIGH).
- Factory forward ('shadow' mode) envelopes can never pass with the shipped --min-trades 100, because gates.py:3053-3055 enforces the 150-trade shadow protocol (executed); the forward-validation stage is dead (HIGH).
- recompute_gate_statistics (gates.py:4315) ignores control.resamples. Once live-shadow tests need more than 20,000 draws (live_shadow_ingest.py:64-70), every passing envelope fails verification (executed: 2.5e-05 versus 5.0e-05), permanently disabling confirmation after roughly 10 LORD++ tests (HIGH).
- For +2 bps net, SD 40 bps and 5 trades/session, the full chain has zero power (two structural blocks) and below 1e-3 even without them. Held-out p <= .05 has power 0.16 and qualification 0.15; BY m=50 cuts the backtest lane to 8.7e-5; the first LORD++ test has power 0.052. MDE is 7.4 bps net for the held-out test alone, 13 bps with BY m=50, and about 26 bps gross under the stress (HIGH).
- The protocol needs at least 150 quote-priced sessions before the backtest lane can pass (last 20% sealed with >= 30 traded sessions), and about 330 sessions (about 16 months) including the forward and live-shadow lanes.
- With 20,000 sign-flip draws the p floor is 5.0e-05, so a global BY batch of 175 or more candidates can never yield a lone discovery (executed); the factory's 12 x 4 x 5 search can reach 240 (MEDIUM).
- Pairing on vehicle:symbol:session silently drops repeat entries within a symbol-session from all paired statistics while coverage still counts them (MEDIUM, LIKELY).
- About 12 of the 31 required checks are implied or vacuous: falsification reuses the held-out p and its ratio test is implied (executed); expectancy equals net; walk-forward passes 99.9% of the time given held-out significance; max_drawdown_supported and fill_quality_adequate always pass for production rows (LOW).
- About 37% of gates.py (verification re-derivation, legacy v2, the reseeded falsification stream, diagnostics and alias keys) serves no promotion decision.
