# momentum.cond.session

Status: candidate
Hypothesis: Momentum expectancy is not uniform across the UTC day and entries taken in thin liquidity are disproportionately stop-runs. Partition executed trades on utc_hour into the asia, thin_hours, europe_us_overlap and off_hours windows pre-registered in research/sweeps/session_conditioning.yaml. Mechanism: a breakout is only information when the level is taken by size the book had to absorb; in the thinnest hours ordinary flow pushes price through the level and back, so the entry prices a liquidity artefact rather than a repricing. Falsified if thin_hours expectancy does not fall below the pooled figure by more than the family-corrected MDE at 100 round trips per window, and no result here may be quoted before it survives the out-of-sample split that spec requires.
