# B7.5 status

B7.5 is the maker-first entry primitive. Exchange and settlement support
exists, and the shipped configuration enables it in `demo` with a 20-second
passive wait before IOC fallback. Configuration validation rejects this path in
`live`. This path is not the `scalp-maker` research strategy.

It completes only when the demo fill/timeout/cancel behavior is covered by
evidence and tests and the resulting execution assumptions are included in the
relevant forward model. Until then, treat the enabled flag as a bounded demo
measurement, not a live capability or promotion. A resting-order or ambiguous
cancel must stop the path rather than fall through to a second order.
