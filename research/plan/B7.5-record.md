# B7.5 status

B7.5 is the maker-first entry primitive. The exchange and settlement support
exists, but the shipped configuration omits `maker_first_enabled`, so config
validation supplies the safe default `false`. This path is not the
`scalp-maker` research strategy and is not active on the demo order path.

It completes when the maker-first configuration is intentionally enabled,
its fill/timeout behavior is covered by evidence and tests, and the resulting
execution assumptions are included in the relevant forward model. Until then,
keep collecting only the data needed to evaluate it; do not treat the dormant
primitive as a live capability.
