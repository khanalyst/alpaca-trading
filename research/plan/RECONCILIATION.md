# Archived evidence reconciliation record

> **ARCHIVED / FROZEN — not a current policy or checklist.** This path remains
> because older code comments and audit artifacts cite it. The policy has been
> consolidated into the canonical lifecycle document.

The durable rule is that recorded, fidelity-checked, paired forward evidence
may support qualification, while recomputed OHLCV evidence is exploratory and
cannot authorize capital. Historical evidence retains its original feed,
schema, code, configuration, corpus, and manifest identity; it is not migrated
into the current population.

Use [`../AUTONOMOUS_RESEARCH.md`](../AUTONOMOUS_RESEARCH.md) for current
authority boundaries, [`../protocol.md`](../protocol.md) for statistical rules,
and [`../../OPERATIONS.md`](../../OPERATIONS.md) for commands and handoff.
