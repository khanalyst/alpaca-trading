# Strategy rationale location

The current strategy definitions, mechanisms, falsifiers, executable triggers,
forward outcome assumptions, evidence status, and parameter settings are
maintained in
[`../HYPOTHESES_AND_VARIANTS.md`](../HYPOTHESES_AND_VARIANTS.md).

This filename is retained as a compatibility pointer for older links. It does
not define a second strategy taxonomy and it is not a source of active strategy
identity.

Research ideas that are not registered strategies are described by their
plain-language implementation artifacts instead:

- volatility-regime conditioning: `research/sweeps/regime_conditioning.yaml`;
- session conditioning: `research/sweeps/session_conditioning.yaml`;
- BTC-residual and concentration diagnostics: journal enrichment and funnel
  analysis;
- maker-first entry measurement: `research/plan/B7.5-record.md`;
- positioning and book-state sample readiness: `research/readiness.py`;
- signal decay and decision cadence: `research.py cadence`.

These are diagnostics, conditioning studies, or execution experiments. They
do not become strategies unless they receive a registered mechanism,
falsifier, executable contract, forward outcome model, settings, and evidence
tier.
