## Design commitment D1, enforced mechanically

Enrichment is journalled and withheld from the prompt. The enforcement is not
a convention: every withheld field lives under an `_enrichment` key, and
`brain.withhold_enrichment()` strips those keys inside `_user_message` on the
way to the provider.

The reason is attribution, not tidiness. Changing the prompt changes model
behaviour, which forks the comparability of every observation recorded before
the change against every one recorded after it. The corpus would split into a
pre-enrichment and a post-enrichment population and every cross-period
comparison in this programme would be contaminated — silently, with no error.

Showing the model open interest is therefore a deliberate, versioned act with
its own batch, its own attribution fork and its own before-and-after replay
diff. It is not a side effect of starting to record.

`tests/research/test_enrichment_isolation.py` holds the boundary, and pins
`prompt_version` to a hand-written constant. A value recomputed from
`build_system()` would agree with itself no matter what the prompt said.

## Failure behaviour

Every field degrades to `None`. Every write is individually wrapped. A raising
book reader, a one-sided book, a rate limit on one symbol, or a snapshot with
no `_enrichment` block at all produces a journalled row of nulls and a
continuing cycle.

This is the acceptance criterion that outranks every field in the batch: no
order depends on any of this, so an observation failure that stopped the agent
trading would be a safety regression introduced by a research feature.
