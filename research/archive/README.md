# Research archive inventory

This directory is the tracked home for small, dated research exports that are
useful as provenance but are not active evidence inputs.

## Historical material

- [`2026-08-07/audit-data/`](2026-08-07/audit-data/) contains the two CSV
  aggregates formerly left in the untracked root `audit-data/` directory.
- [`../legacy/`](../legacy/) contains retained exploratory CLIs and account
  simulations whose reports are already frozen.
- [`../results/`](../results/) contains committed historical reports and their
  manifests.

Historical material is audit-only.  It does not feed the active selector,
qualification, or tournament and cannot raise a strategy tier.

## Active material

The active research path remains in the top-level `research/` modules, notably
`edge_lab.py`, `tournament.py`, `discovery.py`, `protocol.py`, `replay.py`, and
the current staged/realtime evidence paths.  The archive does not replace or
alias those modules.  The identity/load-bearing `maker_study.py` and shared
`signal_lab.py` helpers intentionally remain at their original paths.
