## Standing caveats

- The bars are public consolidated prices from an unofficial endpoint. The official open and close are auction prices; whether a market-on-open or market-on-close order receives exactly the printed open or close is not tested here. The paper trial measures it.
- One cost schedule (September 2026 spreads at 2026 prices) is applied to every year. Spreads were wider in the 2000s, so early-year results are flattered for the high-turnover families.
- The 24-fund universe was chosen in 2026; all its funds survived. Broad index funds rarely close, but the universe is not a point-in-time list.
- Every family and grid was written before the first run, and no protocol constant was changed after it. The catalogue is still the author's choice of hypotheses; a different catalogue gives different multiple-testing hurdles.
- Taxes are ignored. Strategies that trade every session realise only short-term gains.
- Overnight holding carries gap risk that no stop can cap: the position is exposed to everything that happens while the exchange is shut.
- Bills are assumed to be earned on idle cash. The column 'idle cash earns nothing' shows the result if the broker pays no interest.
